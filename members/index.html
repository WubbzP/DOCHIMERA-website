  • Lead member

One of DOCHIMERA's two lead members. WubbzP is a vocal synth cover artist, archiver and visual designer, and runs WubbzP Music, the label DOCHIMERA releases through.

七虹アリスタ

Nanaka Arista

  • Voicebank
  • Birthday: 2 September
  • One-pitch VCV

Her name translates to "Seven Rainbows Arista". "Arista" refers to the brightest star in Virgo, which is associated with a set of seven.

牢人ポラリス

Ronin Polaris

  • Voicebank
  • Birthday: 2 September
  • Pronouns: she/he

"Ronin" refers to its use in Kanaria's エンヴィーベイビー ("Envy Baby"), where it's best translated as "clown" or "pawn". "Polaris" is the North Star, the brightest star in Ursa Minor.

Known as an alternate persona with a distinct, English-leaning way of pronouncing words, Polaris enjoys spending time with other UTAU friends and drinking soft drinks.

Engine
Single-pitch VCV (more pitches planned); single-pitch CV (low quality)
